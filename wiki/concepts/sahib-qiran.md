# The Ṣāḥib-qirān

> *The khan reigns. I conquer. Let no one confuse the two.*

!!! warning "New — largely untested in play"
    The ending, the released state and the fortune have been run with debug tools and corrected after several short tests. No campaign has yet reached this point by play. See [Known issues](../reference/known-issues.md).

At Balkh in April 1370 Temür of the Barlas took the citadel, had his rival Ḥusayn killed, married Ḥusayn's Chinggisid wife Saray Mulk, set Soyurghatmish on the felt and received the oath of the amirs of the ulus one by one. He never took the title of khan. He ruled for thirty-five years as *amīr* and *güregen*, son-in-law of the line, and his panegyrists called him *Ṣāḥib-qirān*, Lord of the Auspicious Conjunction, as they had called great conquerors before him.

What held the amirs to him was not descent and not an office. It was his *dawlat*: the fortune a man is seen to have while he keeps winning and keeps giving. He campaigned almost every year and emptied his treasury on his followers after each campaign. The fortune was his and not his house's, and at his death in 1405 his sons and grandsons fought for four years over a realm none of them had made.

The base game schedules Timur: between 1360 and 1400 an event places the historical man at the head of the Timurid tag around Samarkand. **The mod switches that off.** The conqueror is whoever ends [the Amirs' Regency](amirs-regency.md) as Ṣāḥib-qirān. It may be Temür Barlas. It may be a Qara'unas, a Jalayir, or a darugha with no house at all. If the regency never happens, or ends with the khan triumphant or in the Bulqaq, there is no Timur.

## What changed from the base game

- The base spawn event cannot fire, nor can the two base events that favoured the historical man beforehand (his childhood chain and the "Lame" nickname).
- Temür Barlas still exists as a character at the start, in the **Barlas** dynasty and without the base game's extended life expectancy. He can die young like any other amir's son.
- The base formable **Timurids** (any Uzbek-culture country holding 80% of the Persia region) is closed. The Timurid tag is not a nation to be formed.
- The *Rise of Timur* situation is the mod's own copy with different outcomes, and is shown under the conqueror's name.
- The automatic conquest the base game gives the man for life belongs to his **fortune** here, and is lost when it falls.

## Becoming Ṣāḥib-qirān

In the regency's fourth stage, all of the following at once:

- the regent holds a living khan;
- his ruler has a wife of the line of Chinggis (**Marry into the Line**, or any Borjigin wife);
- the legitimacy gap has stood at 60 or more for **five years running**.

The event *Ṣāḥib-qirān* then reaches him.

## The founding

**The amirs are sorted first**, by what each thinks of the man:

| The house | Becomes |
|-|-|
| Independent, opinion of him 0 or better, bordering him, with its capital in the same area as his | **Annexed outright**, with its men, treasury and people. Temür's own Barlas and the houses around Kesh and Samarkand were his household, not his following |
| Independent, opinion of him 0 or better, anywhere else | A **Sworn Amir** |
| Everyone else | Free. He holds **The Ṣāḥib-qirān's Reckoning** on each for twenty years: taking the amir's seat makes him submit, subjugation is cheap and land is dear |

**Ulus Chaghatay is dissolved.** The khan stays in his tent: a Ṣāḥib-qirān still rules behind one.

**A new state rides out.** If the Timurid tag does not already exist, it is released from all his land with him as ruler, seated at his capital, and his old realm is folded into it. A player is moved to the new state.

| The new state | |
|-|-|
| Name | **Timurids** only if the man is Temür Barlas. Otherwise it is named for his house: the Qara'unas, Barlas, Jalayir, Suldus, Arlat, Apardi, Yasa'uri, Khuttalani, Negüderi, Besüd or Dughlat **Gurkanate**. A man from one of the land-named domains, or with no house, founds **The Ṣāḥib-qirān's Realm** |
| Government | Steppe Horde with Legacy of Genghis and the reform **Güregen of the Ulus**: +0.15 monthly horde unity, +5% land morale, −0.05 monthly prestige, −10% political influence gain, −2% maximum control |
| Culture and religion | His old realm's |
| Rank | Raised to kingdom if lower |
| At once | 1,000 gold, twelve regiments of horse archers and a Kurultai at his seat, all his land made core, +50 horde unity, +25 prestige, 50 political influence (the new state starts with no accepted or tolerated cultures) |
| **Lord of the Conjunction**, ten years | +10% land morale, +0.10 monthly horde unity, +0.10 monthly prestige |

The situation **Rise of** *[his name]* begins for him. His realm is never a member of [the Erānzamīn organisation](eranzamin-organisation.md) or a contender in its succession, and the horde succession poll is not run for it.

## Fortune

The regency's legitimacy gap is not thrown away. It becomes the **Fortune of the Ṣāḥib-qirān**, 0 to 100, shown on the situation's panel. Because the ending requires the gap at 60, he starts in the high band.

| It drains each month | |
|-|-|
| At peace | 0.6 |
| At war | 0.3 |
| After more than three years at peace | 1.2 |

| It is filled by | |
|-|-|
| A great battle won | +4 |
| A siege won | +2 |
| A war won | +10 |
| **Divide the Plunder** | +10 |
| A declared conquest ambition made good | +15 |
| A war lost | −15 |

### The bands

| Fortune | Modifier | Effect |
|-|-|-|
| **60 or more** | **Fortune of the Ṣāḥib-qirān** | +20 subject loyalty, +0.10 monthly horde unity, +5% land morale, −30% antagonism received, +100% loot, less rebel support and −10% separatism in what he takes. **What his armies occupy in a war becomes his at once**, as conquered land |
| 30 to 59 | — | *The amirs wait to see what the next campaign brings* |
| **Under 30** | **A Failing Fortune** | −15 subject loyalty, −0.15 monthly horde unity. His sworn amirs no longer ride unasked |
| **Empty for two years running** | *The Amirs Ride Away* | Every sworn amir breaks free. −25 prestige, −25 horde unity. His fortune is reset to 15 |

Whatever the band, he carries **His Name Goes Before Him**: −60% antagonism received, −20% political influence gain. Temür took Delhi, Damascus and Baghdad and no league ever formed against him; each court dealt with him singly.

### Succession

The fortune is the man's. At every later accession (*The Sons and the Amirs*) the new ruler starts at **25**, in the low band, whatever was left. Once the Conjunction has held (below) heirs start at 50 instead.

## Sworn amirs

A **Sworn Amir** is a follower, not a governor.

| | |
|-|-|
| Diplomatic capacity | None |
| Loyalty | He has none of his own. What holds him is the overlord's fortune: +20 in the high band, −15 in the low |
| Pays | Manpower, not gold |
| War | Rides unasked in every war, offensive and defensive, while the fortune is not low. In the low band he must be called and may refuse |
| Levies | +10% levy size for him; +5% levy size and +0.05 monthly horde unity for the overlord |
| Annexation | Not before 30 years, and only at very high opinion |
| Leaving | He cannot cancel the tie himself and can turn on his overlord only when his loyalty has failed. The overlord can release him (**Release a Sworn Amir**) |

The status exists only by the founding. It cannot be offered or asked for through diplomacy.

## The situation's actions

| Action | What the mod changes |
|-|-|
| **Declare Conquest Ambition** | He names a region. His subjects' borders count as his own, so a region his sworn amirs touch can be named. Nothing obliges him to name Persia first: the AI chooses by the region's wealth against its rulers' strength, counting a realm exhausted by war at half its muster |
| **Plan Invasion** | The neighbours of his subjects are offered as targets as well as his own. When it declares the war, his sworn amirs are put into it as attackers, unless his fortune is low |
| **Divide the Plunder** | New. A year's income, once a year: fortune +10, nobles +5% satisfaction, and **The Plunder Divided** (+10 subject loyalty) for three years |
| The reform action that settles the horde | It becomes a [Persianate Sultanate](persianate-sultanate.md) with the **Timurid Empire** reform, Persian as court language, empire rank and designation-then-contest succession (see [Government reforms](government-reforms.md)) |

The base event *Our Legacy*, which asks the conqueror where his authority comes from, is not fired: he already rules as *güregen* behind a khan.

Divide the Plunder needs the situation to be running. A Ṣāḥib-qirān whose realm was not released as the Timurid tag (because that tag already existed) has the fortune but not the button.

## How the situation ends

| Outcome | Condition | Result |
|-|-|-|
| **The Conjunction Holds** | Two declared conquest ambitions made good — he and his subjects hold six in ten locations of each region — while he still owns the seat his rule began from and his fortune is not low | +50 prestige. The fortune continues, and from now on each heir starts at 50 |
| **The Amirs Ride Away** | The fortune has lain empty for two years and the sworn amirs have gone | The campaign bonus lapses and outstanding claims of conquest expire |
| **The Sons Divide** | The founder is dead and an heir's fortune has stayed under 30 for five years running; or the realm has neither ruler nor heir | The same |
| The realm is subjugated | As in the base game | — |

The ambitions may be any regions: Iran, Hindustan, the Qipchaq steppe, China.

### The army is lost

Once in a game. If the Ṣāḥib-qirān loses a war to an outsider while his fortune is already under 30, the sworn amirs ride away at once (−25 prestige, −25 horde unity), and he and they are treated as the orphans of a broken ulus: every outside neighbour holds **Break the Tümens** on each for 25 years, the victor holds it whether he borders them or not, and [the Breaking of the Chaghatay](amirs-regency.md#the-breaking-of-the-chaghatay) opens as after a Bulqaq.

## Playing it

**As the Ṣāḥib-qirān.** You are not running an empire yet; you are keeping a following. Stay at war: fortune drains at half speed in a war and at double speed after three years of peace. Keep it at 60 and occupied land becomes yours without a peace treaty, your amirs ride without being asked, and other courts barely register your conquests. Let it fall under 30 and the amirs must be asked, and a lost war can end everything. Divide the plunder every year you can afford it. Name a conquest ambition your amirs' borders reach, take six tenths of the region, then name another: two made good, with your first seat still yours, makes the house last. Your heir starts at 25 unless you have done that.

**Against him.** While his fortune is high you cannot buy time by letting him occupy land. He is weakest at peace and under an heir. Beat him in a war when his fortune is low and his following dissolves.

**The AI** does not march west by rule. It names the richest region whose rulers it can beat, sends embassies and gifts to neighbours clearly stronger than itself, and falls on a great realm that has just been bled.
