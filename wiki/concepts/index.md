# Concepts

The core mechanics added by the mod. Each page opens with the historical frame and then describes what the game actually does with it.

Start with the **government** and the **dyad** — almost every other mechanic in the mod reads or writes them.

## The political order

- [**The Persianate Sultanate**](persianate-sultanate.md) — the baseline government, replacement for the European monarchy slot on the Iranian plateau and across the Persianate sphere.
- [**Türk vs Tāzīk**](turk-vs-tazik.md) — the men of the sword and the men of the pen; the axis that gates reforms, privileges, and tribal loyalty.
- [**The Erānzamīn succession situation**](eranzamin-situation.md) — the realm-wide crisis triggered by Abū Saʿīd's death and the extinction of the Hülegüid line.
- [**The Ilkhanate Governorate**](ilkhanate-governorate.md) — the deferral: claim nothing, administer your corner, and swear to whoever wins.

## The two elites

- [**The Dīwān estate**](diwan-estate.md) — the Persian scribal class, the chancery, the waqf endowments, the ledger.
- [**The Amirs estate**](amirs-estate.md) — the Turco-Mongol military aristocracy, the iqṭāʿ holders, the tribal *noyans*.
- [**Estate privileges**](estate-privileges.md) — rivalry pairs, prerogative violations, and the price the other side charges for each.

## Statecraft

- [**Government reforms (Ages 1–3)**](government-reforms.md) — the Age 1 choice (Regency / Caliphal Investiture / Royal Farr, plus the [Governorate](ilkhanate-governorate.md) deferral) and what flows out of each branch.
- [**The Farr Proof of Fortune**](farr-proof-of-fortune.md) — the disaster that follows the `farr_i_izadi` claim: the wager, the apparatus claims, the four outcomes.
- [**The Ghāzī Burden of the Sword**](ghazi-burden-of-sword.md) — the disaster that follows `ghazi_sultanate`: war-credit tiers, the *muḥtasib* axis, internal campaigns, the four outcomes.
- [**The Borrowed Mandate**](borrowed-mandate.md) — the disaster that follows `chinggisid_regency`: rival khans, the mortal succession, the Sole Custody fork, and the Gurkani exit.
- [**The Matter of Tork**](matter-of-tork.md) — the late disaster every non-Borjigin consolidator faces: what becomes of the Türk military class. Four approaches, five dispositions, the dehqān refounding.
- [**The Mazālim court**](mazalim-court.md) — the parliament slot occupied by the king's own court of grievances, and the crown decrees that issue from it.
- [**Buildings of the pen and the sword**](buildings.md) — the three-tier Dīwān chain and the iqṭāʿ grant.
- [**The military system**](military-system.md) — iqṭāʿ, tribal levies, ghulāms, and the absence of the European siege game.
