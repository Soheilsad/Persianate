# The Ilkhanate Governorate — the deferral

> *We are governors, not kings. Let the others break themselves on the throne.*

Not every house of the old Ilkhanate reached for the throne. The Injuids held Fars as the fiscal governors they had been under Abū Saʿīd; the Kart *maliks* kept Herat as they had kept it under four Ilkhans; a dozen lesser dynasties were, quite literally, the provincial administration carrying on after the centre had gone. They read aloud whatever name the strongest neighbour sent them, kept their registers, and waited to see who would win. The mod makes that a deliberate posture: the fourth answer to the opening question of [the Succession of Erānzamīn](eranzamin-situation.md), beside the regency, the *ghazā* and the *farr*.

## Taking the road

At the opening event, *Hulagu's Seat Stands Empty*, the governorate is the answer **"We are governors, not kings."** It adopts the **Ilkhanate Governorate** reform and gives +5 legitimacy and +5% Dīwān satisfaction: the chancery approves of the caution, and nobody is provoked, because nothing has been claimed.

The AI defers according to its strength. The weakest realms take this road overwhelmingly; a realm with a real army rarely does. A realm that cannot field a host has no business claiming the *ulus*.

The reform is also on the reform panel for any member of [the Erānzamīn organisation](eranzamin-organisation.md) that holds none of the rival answers (the regency, Farr-i Īzadī, Ghāzī Sultanate, Amīr Sultanate, Sword of The Sharia) and is not a *ṭarīqa* order. It takes effect at once.

## The reform

The administrator who keeps his head down: defensible, solvent and unthreatening.

| Effect | Value | Reading |
|-|-|-|
| Monthly legitimacy | +0.1 | a recognised governor's lawful place — modest, and no more |
| Stability cost efficiency | +10% | steady |
| Fort defence | +20% | defensible in your corner — worth more than it looks while the region's walls stand at −66% |
| Land morale | +5% | |
| Tax efficiency | +10% | a well-run province |
| Antagonism received | −20% | nobody marches on the humble governor |
| Monthly prestige | −0.1 | a governor is not a king |
| Province integration speed | −20% | no reach |
| Diplomatic reputation | −1 | |

A governorate counts as having answered the legitimacy question: it does not suffer **Unsettled Legitimacy**. If it is independent, Muslim and seated in the region it is still a contender in the eyes of the situation — it keeps the contender's modifier, the sword rule and the situation's actions — but in the organisation's ledger it holds no role. Unless it is allied to a bloc head it appears under *Unaligned*, among the "governors waiting to see who wins".

## Swearing to a steward

A governorate can read a stronger man's khan in its khuṭba and become his **sworn governorate**. There are three ways this happens, and they all end in the same bond.

| Path | Who starts it | How it works |
|-|-|-|
| **Declare Fealty** | the governorate | an action on the situation panel, available to an independent governorate at peace. You choose a steward from the roll of members who hold the Chinggisid Regency, are ruled by a Borjigin, or are contending in earnest. It takes effect at once: the steward is informed (*The Sermon Is Already Read*) and cannot refuse. |
| *In Whose Name Do We Govern?* | the times | while at least one regency stands, an independent governorate is pressed to choose — at most once every five years, with roughly a one-in-eleven chance each month it is due. It is offered the **three strongest regencies** by military strength, or to hold out: *"Not yet. We were made governors by Abū Saʿīd."* Swearing gives +5 legitimacy. |
| **Demand Fealty** | a regent | a regent under [the Borrowed Mandate](borrowed-mandate.md) sends robes and gifts and calls the governorate to swear. Accept, and you receive gold equal to his tax base; refuse, and you keep your corner and your quarrel. |

Note the difference in who may be sworn to: the periodic event offers only regencies, while **Declare Fealty** also lets you kneel to a Borjigin sovereign or to any contender — a *farr* claimant or a *ghāzī* included.

### What the steward gets

For a regent the oath is mostly titular, but the titular part is his whole case: while he rules as a Chinggisid Regency, **each sworn governorate gives him +0.05 monthly legitimacy and +0.10 monthly political influence**. For any overlord, the governorate's land counts toward his bloc in the organisation's ledger and toward the 400 locations the throne requires — though one of the Five Seats held by a governorate does not count as his until he owns it himself.

## The sworn governorate as a subject

This is not a conquered vassal. It is a deferring administrator who chose the arrangement and keeps his own house. In the game it is named **Ilkhanate Governorate** in the subject list, and its overlord is the **Liege of the Governorates**.

| Term | Detail |
|-|-|
| How it is created | only by the three paths above — it cannot be demanded in a peace treaty or offered through ordinary diplomacy |
| Tribute | pays as an ordinary vassal does |
| Its ruler | its own; the overlord's ruler does not take the seat |
| The overlord's wars | the overlord **may call** it to his offensive and defensive wars; it does not join automatically |
| Its own wars | **it may declare war on anyone except its overlord** — fellow governorates above all, and outsiders too |
| Its defence | the overlord is **not** drawn in automatically. When a sworn governorate is attacked by anyone else, the overlord is asked (*The Governorate Calls*): ride and join the war, or refuse at a cost of 5 legitimacy and the governorate's resentment |
| Building | the overlord cannot build roads, buildings or resource works in its land |
| Annexation | not before 15 years, only at an opinion of 175, and at half the usual speed |
| Release | the overlord may cancel the bond |
| Diplomacy | limited; its diplomatic capacity cost to the overlord is scaled by 0.75 |
| Rank and succession | it may change its own rank and its own heir selection |

The right to fight one's fellow governorates is the heart of it. The Ilkhanid successor melee was largely a war among men who all read the same khan's name on Friday; the one bond the governorate keeps is that name, and everything else it settles by the sword.

## Dropping the khan

The way out is **Drop the Khan from the Khutbah**, an action on the situation panel for a sworn governorate that is not at war with its overlord. It is a renunciation, not a negotiation:

| Effect | Detail |
|-|-|
| The bond | ends at once; any truce with the former overlord is removed |
| The former overlord | takes offence (*Ignored the hallowed bloodline of Genghis Khan*) and receives a **Succession of Erānzamīn** casus belli against you |
| Your reform | the **Ilkhanate Governorate** is removed |
| Your next answer | *Hulagu's Seat Stands Empty* is put to you again — and for five years the governorate is not among the answers. Having dropped a khan, you must pick a side: the regency, the *ghazā* or the *farr* |
| Legitimacy | +5 |

An AI governorate walks out when it has grown strong enough to stand alone, or stronger than the overlord who was meant to shield it.

## Practical advice

- **For a small realm this is the safest opening in the game.** +20% fort defence and −20% antagonism under a reform that provokes no one, and the option to buy a protector later with a single action.
- **Swear when you are threatened, not before.** An independent governorate keeps its full freedom of action. Declare Fealty is instant and cannot be refused, so it can be held in reserve until the army is at the gates — but it requires you to be at peace, so do not wait for the declaration of war itself.
- **Choose a steward who will ride.** The overlord's defence is a decision, not a guarantee. A regent already at war is much less likely to come.
- **Use the licence to fight.** As a sworn governorate you can still make war on every neighbour except your overlord, with his name over your door.
- **The road has no summit of its own.** The reform's ceilings on prestige, integration and reputation are not built for a realm gathering 400 locations and five great cities. Sooner or later a growing governorate drops the khan and answers the question properly; plan for the casus belli your former overlord receives the day you do.
- **For regents: governorates are legitimacy.** See [The Borrowed Mandate](borrowed-mandate.md) for the price of asking and the value of each oath.
