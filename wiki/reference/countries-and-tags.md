# Countries and tags

A reference to the countries the mod adds or changes at the 1337 start: what each is called in game, its tag, where it sits, how it is governed on day one, and which page of this wiki covers it. Countries the mod leaves exactly as the base game has them are listed only where they belong to one of the mod's organisations.

"Türk-leaning" and "Tāzīk-leaning" give the starting position on [the Türk–Tāzīk axis](../concepts/turk-vs-tazik.md): −25 or +25, with the matching set of starting privileges. Location counts are those owned on day one. A dash for the ruler means the game generates one.

### Members the mod leaves as they are

These base-game countries are members of Erānzamīn with no change beyond their starting culture lists: Artaz (ATZ), Khalkhal (KKL — it owns the town of Ardabil), the Dilmaçids (DIL, a Sutayid vassal), the Artuqids (ATQ), the Ayyubids of Ḥiṣn Kayfā (HSN), Georgia (GEO) with Alastani (AAI), Shaki (SKI) and Samtskhe (SME), and Armenia (ARM) with Khachen (KCN), Syunik (SYU) and Vayots Dzor (VDZ). The Christian realms are members but not contenders.

## The *ṭarīqa* orders

Landless shrine-polities: each has its seat in a city it does not own, a Grand Khānaqāh there, and Tariqa Lodges in two other towns. All use the Persianate Sultanate government with the **Tariqa Order** reform, at county rank. They are neither members of Erānzamīn nor contenders. See [Tariqa orders](../concepts/tariqa-orders.md).

| Order | Tag | Seat | Shaykh at start | Lodges at start |
|-|-|-|-|-|
| Ardabil (the Safaviyya) | ABL | Ardabil | Ṣadr al-Dīn Mūsā | Tabriz, Ahar |
| Kubrawiyya | KBW | Semnan | Maḥmūd Mazdaqānī | Damghan, Nishapur |
| Kazaruniyya | KZR | Kazerun | Amīn al-Dīn Balyānī | Shiraz, Siraf |
| Qadiriyya | QAD | Baghdad | Sharaf al-Dīn Aḥmad Jīlānī | Mosul, Samarra |
| Khwajagan | KWJ | Bukhara | Muḥammad Bābā Sammāsī | Samarkand, Karmana |
| Yasaviyya | YSV | Turkestan (Yasi) | Aḥmad Ātā | Otrar, Sawran |
| Rifaiyya | RIF | Wasit | ʿIzz al-Dīn Aḥmad Rifāʿī | Basra, Hillah |

Ardabil is a base-game tag; there it is a small landed state. In the mod it is the Safavid order — Sunni at the start, and seated in a town that belongs to Khalkhal.

## A note on names

Hamedān, Kāshān, Esfarāyen, Qom, Merv and Kuhistān take their country names from the city or district they hold, spelled as the game spells the place. Rulers are given here in their usual scholarly form. The game builds character names from its own name list, so a few read differently on screen — Ardashīr of Shabankara, for one, appears under the Greek form of his name.
