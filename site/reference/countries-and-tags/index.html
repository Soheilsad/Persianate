    Countries and tags¶

    A reference to the countries the mod adds or changes at the 1337 start: what each is called in game, its tag, where it sits, how it is governed on day one, and which page of this wiki covers it. Countries the mod leaves exactly as the base game has them are listed only where they belong to one of the mod's organisations.

    "Türk-leaning" and "Tāzīk-leaning" give the starting position on the Türk–Tāzīk axis: −25 or +25, with the matching set of starting privileges. Location counts are those owned on day one. A dash for the ruler means the game generates one.

    The successor states of the Ilkhanate¶

    All are members of the Erānzamīn organisation, and every independent Muslim one is a contender in the Succession of Erānzamīn. The opening map is described on Starting the game.

    Members the mod leaves as they are¶

    These base-game countries are members of Erānzamīn with no change beyond their starting culture lists: Artaz (ATZ), Khalkhal (KKL — it owns the town of Ardabil), the Dilmaçids (DIL, a Sutayid vassal), the Artuqids (ATQ), the Ayyubids of Ḥiṣn Kayfā (HSN), Georgia (GEO) with Alastani (AAI), Shaki (SKI) and Samtskhe (SME), and Armenia (ARM) with Khachen (KCN), Syunik (SYU) and Vayots Dzor (VDZ). The Christian realms are members but not contenders.

    The ṭarīqa orders¶

    Landless shrine-polities: each has its seat in a city it does not own, a Grand Khānaqāh there, and Tariqa Lodges in two other towns. All use the Persianate Sultanate government with the Tariqa Order reform, at county rank. They are neither members of Erānzamīn nor contenders. See Tariqa orders.

    Order Tag Seat Shaykh at start Lodges at start

    Kazaruniyya KZR Kazerun Amīn al-Dīn Balyānī Shiraz, Siraf
    Qadiriyya QAD Baghdad Sharaf al-Dīn Aḥmad Jīlānī Mosul, Samarra
    Khwajagan KWJ Bukhara Muḥammad Bābā Sammāsī Samarkand, Karmana

    Ardabil is a base-game tag; there it is a small landed state. In the mod it is the Safavid order — Sunni at the start, and seated in a town that belongs to Khalkhal.

    Ulus Chaghatay¶

    The Chagatai khanate is rebuilt as an organisation: the khan leads, fifteen amirs are junior members with the status Amir of the Ulus, and two settled dynasties sit in it as Client Kings of the Ulus. None of them are members of Erānzamīn. The khan's seat, the eight western houses and the four new domains are of Chaghatay culture. See Ulus Chaghatay, The Amirs' Regency and Hordes.

    The Timurid tag (TIM) does not appear by date and cannot be formed. It is released for whoever ends the Amirs' Regency as Ṣāḥib-qirān, and carries the name Timurids only if that man is Temür Barlas; otherwise it is named for his house (for example the Qara'unas Gurkanate) or The Ṣāḥib-qirān's Realm. See The Ṣāḥib-qirān.

    The Persianate periphery¶

    Two great sultanates outside Iran are moved onto the Persianate Sultanate government, with a standing chancery and iqṭāʿ network on the map from day one. Neither belongs to Erānzamīn.

    Country Tag Seat Government at start Ruler Start situation
    Egypt MAM Cairo Persianate Sultanate; kingdom rank al-Nāṣir Muḥammad Territory as in the base game. Dīwān of the Realms in Cairo, Regional Dīwān in Damascus, Local Dīwāns in Aleppo and Alexandria; nine Iqṭāʿ Grants. Overlord of Āl Faḍl.
    Delhi DLH Sargadwari Persianate Sultanate, The Delhi Dispensation; kingdom rank Muḥammad b. Tughluq Territory as in the base game. Dīwān of the Realms in Delhi, Regional Dīwāns in Daulatabad and Multan; eight Iqṭāʿ Grants.

    A note on names¶

    Hamedān, Kāshān, Esfarāyen, Qom, Merv and Kuhistān take their country names from the city or district they hold, spelled as the game spells the place. Rulers are given here in their usual scholarly form. The game builds character names from its own name list, so a few read differently on screen — Ardashīr of Shabankara, for one, appears under the Greek form of his name.
