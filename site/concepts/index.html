    Concepts¶

    The mechanics added by the mod. Each page opens with the historical frame and then describes what the game does with it.

    Start with the government and the dyad of Türk and Tāzīk — almost every other mechanic in the mod reads or writes them.

    The Persianate state¶

    • The Persianate Sultanate — the baseline government of the Persianate sphere.
    • Government reforms — the complete list of reforms, by age.
    • Türk vs Tāzīk — the men of the sword and the men of the pen.
    • The Dīwān estate — the scribal class, the chancery, the ledger.
    • The Amirs estate — the military aristocracy, the iqṭāʿ holders, their private regiments.
    • Estate privileges — the complete catalogue, and political influence.
    • The Maẓālim court — the parliament slot, held by the ruler's court of grievances.
    • Buildings, goods and materials — the complete building catalogue.
    • The military system — levies, units and the three forces.
    • Demography and cultures — the population of 1337 and the starting culture lists.

    The succession of Erānzamīn¶

    • The Succession of Erānzamīn — the crisis that follows the extinction of the Hülegüid line.
    • The Erānzamīn organisation — its panel, its members and their roles.
    • The Borrowed Mandate — the Custody of the Ulus: rule in a khan's name.
    • The Ilkhanate Governorate — the deferral.
    • The Farr Under Examination — the audit that follows the claim to farr-i īzadī.
    • The Burden of the Sword — the audit that follows the Ghāzī Sultanate.
    • The Matter of Tork — the place of the Türk military class in a consolidated realm.

    The Shia world¶

    • The Commonwealth of Martyrs — the Sarbadar government and its two estates.
    • Ṭarīqa orders and the Shia rework — aspects, establishments, the orders and their rising.
    • The Zaydi Imamate and Yemen — the imamate organisation and the rebuilt Yemen.

    The steppe¶

    • Hordes — the yam, Darugha's Seats, ulus grants and Törü succession.
    • Ulus Chaghatay — the Chagatai khanate as an organisation: weight, pledges, risings, raids, client kings.
    • The Amirs' Regency — the disaster carried by whoever holds the khan, its four stages and three endings.
    • The Ṣāḥib-qirān — the emergent Timur, his fortune and his sworn amirs.
    • The Beglerbeg's Regency — a beg on the Golden Horde's throne, and the princes against him.

    The periphery¶

    • Shattered Rum — the Anatolian beyliks and the Seljuk Inheritance.
    • Kurdish emirates — the Iranic Tribal Polity.
    • Periphery sultanates — Egypt, Delhi and the Indian successor states.
