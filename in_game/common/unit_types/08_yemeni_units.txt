# =====================================================================
# YEMENI HIGHLAND WARFARE — the imam's army.
#
# The Zaydi imamate never fielded a lowland army. Its force was the
# qabili: the armed highland tribesman of Hashid, Bakil and Khawlan,
# a terrace farmer with spear, sword, shield and jambiya, fighting from
# fortress-villages on 2,000-3,000 m ridges. He is nearly unbeatable on
# his own ground and of little use on the Tihama plain, which is why
# every lowland state from the Rasulids to the Ottomans held the coast
# and lost the mountains. Horse was scarce: the sayyid houses' retinues
# and the Jawf and Najran tribes, camel-and-horse country on the
