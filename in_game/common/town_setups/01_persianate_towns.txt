# ============================================================
# Tiny town variants — the qaṣaba scale
#
# The Iranian / Iraqi / Transoxianan world had hundreds of
# qaṣabas below the rank of a real town: administrative seats,
# road halts, frontier outposts, shrine villages. The base game
# maps Latin Europe at this density (every cathedral town, every
# market lord's seat) and the Persianate world deserves the same
# fabric. These templates carry it: light on the luxury crafts,
# but with the literacy infrastructure (the maktab, a modest
# scriptorium) that the Islamic urban tradition extended down to
# even small places.
# ============================================================
