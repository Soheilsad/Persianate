# =====================================================================
# WHAT AN ULUS PAYS — men, not money.
#
# This is the exact inverse of the Rus tributary, and the contrast is
# the point. A Rus prince held his throne by a revocable patent, paid
# the vykhod in silver, kept his own law and a tax-exempt church, and
# was not family. What he owed was money, and what he cared about was
# whether the tümens would actually come if he stopped paying.
#
# An ulus holder owes the opposite. He is a shareholder in the
# patrimony, not a taxpayer on it — his right to his pastures and the
# khan's right to the seat come from the same source, the estate of the
# line. No basqaq counts his herds and no silver goes to Sarai. What he
# owes is his tümens at the muster and his presence at the quriltai.
#
# So: manpower and sailors flow up at a real rate, gold at a token one.
# The 0.02 is not zero because a share of plunder and the customary
# gifts did move toward the senior khan — but a khan who grants ulus to
# solve his reach problem must not be solving his revenue problem at
# the same time. The whole trade is meant to be reach and soldiers
# bought with legitimacy and future civil war, never with cash.
#
# Compare base: subject_pays_vassal 0.2 gold, subject_pays_tributary
# 0.2 gold + 0.05 men, subject_pays_march 0.1 gold and no men at all.
# =====================================================================

ulus_pays = {
	scaled_gold = 0.05
	scaled_manpower = 0.25
	scaled_sailors = 0.1
	ignore_inflation = yes
}
